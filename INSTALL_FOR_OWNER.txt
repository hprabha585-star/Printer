===========================================================
 SETTING UP YOUR SHOP COMPUTER TO PRINT ORDERS AUTOMATICALLY
===========================================================

You only need to do this ONCE. It takes about 2 minutes.

WHAT YOU NEED FROM YOUR DEVELOPER:
  1. Your website address        (e.g. https://yourshop.com)
  2. Your connector password     (a one-time password to sign the
                                   printer in — you can change it later)

STEPS:

  1. Get the file "PrintShopConnector.exe" from your developer and put
     it anywhere on your computer's Desktop.

  2. Double-click it. A small window titled "Print Shop Connector"
     will open.

  3. Type your website address into the first box.

  4. Type your connector password into the second box.

  5. Click the red "Connect" button.

  6. You should see "Connected — watching for print jobs" in green.
     That's it — you're done!

  7. (Optional, recommended) Tick the box that says "Start
     automatically when this computer turns on" and click Connect
     again. Now you'll never have to open this yourself — it starts
     working as soon as you turn the computer on.

EVERY DAY AFTER THIS:
  - If you ticked the auto-start box, do nothing — it's already running.
  - Otherwise, just double-click the same icon each morning and click
    Connect. Leave the window open (you can minimize it) while the
    shop is open. New orders will print automatically and you'll see
    them listed in the window as they happen.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG:
  - "Connection problem" or a red message — check your internet
    connection, then click Disconnect and Connect again.
  - "Password not accepted" — the password may have been changed in
    your Owner Dashboard. Open the dashboard on your phone or computer
    at yourwebsite.com/owner, sign in, and check Settings.
  - Nothing prints even though it says Connected — make sure your
    printer is turned on and set as the "default printer" in Windows
    (Settings > Printers & Scanners).

You can always reach your developer if anything here doesn't match
what you're seeing.
